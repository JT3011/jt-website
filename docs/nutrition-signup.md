## Individual client prices

JT can open `/nutrition-pricing.html` directly or use **Nutrition prices** from the owner Hub toolbar. Separate programme quotes appear under Support and are explicitly separate from the GBP 12.99 Hub membership. Enter the client’s sign-up email, a GBP amount, one-off/monthly basis and a description. Clients see only an active quote whose email exactly matches their verified Supabase account email. Email aliases are not merged. An existing quote can be edited or hidden; changing its email requires a new quote.

The editor saves quotes only. It does not take payments, start subscriptions, change existing Stripe charges or grant paid membership. Custom programme payment remains arranged with JT; the standard Hub subscription is collected through the existing Stripe link above.

The API is under `/api/hub-session-plans?programme=nutrition-pricing`. Owner authorization is checked on the server, without trusting browser role metadata. Direct browser reads/writes to `hub_nutrition_quotes` are revoked and RLS is enabled. The client lookup uses the server-verified email, not a supplied email parameter. Updates use a revision predicate to reject stale edits. No real client prices were created while developing this feature.

Validation: `node --test tests/nutrition-pricing.test.mjs tests/nutrition-signup.test.mjs tests/personal-programmes.test.mjs` passes 32 tests. `tests/nutrition-pricing-rls.sql` passes against the deployed schema and rolls back its synthetic row. The editor, update and client display were also exercised in a local browser fixture.

## Weekly planner and kitchen

The dedicated nutrition workspace now includes a dated weekly planner, a personal recipe collection with favourites/search/quick filters, approved meal swaps, moving meals between days, household serving controls, step-by-step cook mode and a foreground kitchen timer. Clients can save the shared rotation, plan up to 12 weeks ahead, copy into an empty future week and revisit saved weeks from the past year. A new shared plan can replace the current week's older plan. The authored coach plan is never overwritten.

Shopping is grouped by aisle. Clients enter cupboard quantities and tick off items, copy the remaining list or print it. Quantities and whole-pack estimates are recalculated from actual scheduled meals, serving counts and cupboard stock. Collecting an item does not remove its cost from the basket total. Swaps/serving changes clear shopping ticks; moving meals preserves them; copied weeks start with no assumed cupboard stock or collected items. Unknown prices remain unknown. Store links open the verified source and do not create supermarket baskets or orders.

`hub_nutrition_planner` stores each client's favourites and dated weeks with optimistic revisions. The existing player/linked-parent/assigned-coach/owner access rules apply; direct client writes are revoked and RLS protects reads. API mutations validate published plan ownership, meal slots, approved recipes, portions, ingredient IDs and current questionnaire revision. JT AI Coach reads only the signed-in client's saved current week and re-checks its referenced plan is published. It cannot edit the planner.

Apply `supabase/jt-nutrition-planner.sql` (applied as `nutrition_planner_weeks_favourites_shopping` on 25 November 2026). `tests/nutrition-planner-rls.sql` checks own versus unrelated reads and revoked writes inside a rolled-back transaction. `tests/nutrition-planner.test.mjs` covers weekly calculations, pantry/pack totals, approved swaps, copied weeks, stale revisions and access. No Whipp assets, recipes, branding or integration are used; the UX was informed by its public feature descriptions at https://www.whipp.app/ and https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/whipp-meal-planner-recipes/id6778126199.
