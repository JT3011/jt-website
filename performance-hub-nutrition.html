Football Fuel Timing

Keep It Practical.

Familiar foods and regular habits usually beat complicated plans. Use these four checkpoints as a simple match and training framework.

01

Earlier In The Day

Build a familiar meal with carbohydrate, protein, colour and fluids rather than trying to catch up immediately before football.

02

Before Football

Choose comfortable foods you know you tolerate. A simple carbohydrate-rich snack may be useful when there is a long gap between meals and activity.

03

During Activity

Use breaks as a reminder to drink. Longer sessions, warmer conditions and heavier sweating increase the importance of regular fluids.

04

After Football

Follow activity with fluids and a meal or snack containing carbohydrate and protein, then return to your normal eating pattern.

Professional Standards

Small Habits. Often.

Eat Regularly Regular meals and snacks can make it easier to arrive at training with energy and concentration.
Use Variety Rotate food choices across the week instead of chasing one supposedly perfect meal or ingredient.
Prepare Early Pack food, bottles and matchday options before the schedule becomes busy.
Your development goal:
Hydration Check

Arrive Ready.

Start Across The Day Drinking steadily is usually more practical than trying to consume a large amount just before training or kick-off.
Bring Your Bottle Keep fluids available at school, work, training and matches so the habit does not depend on memory alone.
Continue Afterwards Recovery continues after the final whistle. Resume normal drinking and eating rather than stopping when the session ends.
